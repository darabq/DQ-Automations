How It Works

Set up once. Runs forever.

You don't touch anything. It just works.

1
Someone calls your business number and you can't answer
Instead of going to voicemail, they instantly receive a professional SMS from your business saying you saw their call and will be in touch shortly.
2
They reply with what they need
The AI reads their reply, understands the urgency, and logs it automatically. Every future message to that person references their specific situation.
3
You wake up to a prioritized list
Your Google Sheet shows every missed call sorted by urgency — as determined by AI. High urgency at the top. You know exactly who to call first.
4
You call them back. Text DONE.
When you've dealt with someone, text DONE and their number to your business line. They're marked as called back instantly. No app. No login. Just a text.
5
2AM: the system cleans itself
Every night at 2AM, completed calls are archived automatically. You wake up to a clean, accurate sheet every single morning.
