Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · DQ Automations Inc. · Mississauga, Ontario, Canada

DQ Automations Inc. is committed to protecting the privacy of our clients and their customers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA).

1. Who We Are

DQ Automations Inc. is an Ontario corporation (Corporation No. 1791830-5) providing business automation services to small and medium-sized businesses in the Greater Toronto Area and beyond. We are headquartered in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada.

Contact: owner@dqautomations.com · 647-863-2557

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two contexts: from our direct clients (businesses), and from end users (their customers who interact with our automated systems).

From Clients (Businesses):

  • Business name, address, and contact information
  • Phone numbers and email addresses
  • Business registration and tax information
  • Payment information (processed securely via Stripe)
  • API credentials and system access information

From End Users (Customers of our clients):

  • Phone numbers (when they call a business using our system)
  • SMS message content (when they reply to automated messages)
  • General nature of their inquiry (as interpreted by AI)
  • Time and date of contact

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide automated missed call follow-up and invoice tracking services
  • To send SMS messages on behalf of our clients
  • To log and prioritize incoming inquiries in Google Sheets
  • To process payments and manage billing
  • To comply with carrier regulations and applicable law

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Platforms

Our services operate through the following third-party platforms, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Telnyx — telecommunications and SMS delivery
  • Make.com (Celonis) — workflow automation
  • Anthropic (Claude) — AI processing of message content
  • Google (Workspace & Sheets) — data storage and processing
  • Stripe — payment processing

By using our services, you acknowledge that data flows through these platforms in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We use Anthropic's API with zero data retention settings where available, meaning message content is not used to train AI models.

5. SMS Communications & Opt-Out

End users who contact a business using our system may receive automated SMS follow-up messages. These messages are sent on behalf of our clients, not directly by DQ Automations Inc.

  • To stop receiving messages, reply STOP to any message
  • To resume messages, reply START
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us at support@dqautomations.com
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • Message frequency varies by business

6. Data Retention

Client data is stored within Google Sheets accounts maintained by DQ Automations Inc. on behalf of each client. Upon termination of services:

  • System access is revoked within 5 business days
  • Client data is permanently deleted within 30 days of termination
  • Data may be retained longer if required by law or tax regulations

7. Data Security

We employ reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information, including password protection and two-factor authentication on all accounts. In the event of a data breach, affected parties will be notified as soon as reasonably practicable and within 72 hours of confirmation.

8. Your Rights (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), you have the right to:

  • Access personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent for the use of your information
  • File a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada

To exercise these rights, contact us at owner@dqautomations.com.

9. Cookies

Our website (dqautomations.com) does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. No personal information is collected through website visits.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
